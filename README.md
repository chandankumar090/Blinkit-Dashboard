# Blinkit Grocery Sales Analytics Dashboard 📊🛒

A comprehensive, interactive **Power BI Analytics Dashboard** designed to evaluate operational performance, sales optimization strategies, and retail distribution trends for Blinkit (India's Last Minute App). This project processes grocery retail data to track crucial business targets, examine inventory variety, and map out grocery outlet effectiveness using advanced business intelligence tools.

---

## 🚀 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard provides a high-level summary of grocery business health across **four core metrics**:
* **Total Sales:** \$1.20M in aggregate revenue generated.
* **Average Sales:** \$141 per transaction value.
* **Number of Items:** 8,523 unique inventory items tracked.
* **Average Rating:** 3.9 out of 5 stars for customer feedback.

---

## 📈 Visualizations & Features
The dashboard incorporates diverse data visualization techniques to capture multi-dimensional retail trends:

* **Outlet Establishment Growth:** A chronological area chart mapping revenue peaks over time, showcasing a major historic peak in **2018 at \$204.52K**.
* **Sales Distribution by Fat Content:** A localized donut chart outlining consumer inventory choices, demonstrating **\$776K** spent on Regular items versus **\$425K** spent on Low Fat products.
* **Item Type Revenue Performance:** A detailed horizontal bar chart breaking down sales by department, led by **Fruits and Vegetables (\$0.18M)** and **Snack Foods (\$0.18M)**.
* **Outlet Characteristics Analysis:**
  * *Outlet Size:* A ring chart comparing Medium (**\$508K**), Small (**\$445K**), and High (**\$249K**) store formats.
  * *Outlet Location Tiering:* A stacked horizontal breakdown tracking performance across regional tiers, led by **Tier 3 cities (\$472.13K)**.
* **Outlet Type Performance Matrix:** A comprehensive matrix visual correlating Supermarket Types and Grocery Stores with deep metrics across Total Sales, Item Count, Average Ratings, and Item Visibility factors.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filter Panel (Slicers)
To allow users to easily drill down into specific data points, the dashboard includes dynamic, cross-filtering slicers in a unified left panel:
* **Outlet Location Type:** Filter analytics by specific urban tiers (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3).
* **Outlet Size:** Instantly filter metrics by High, Medium, or Small locations.
* **Item Type:** Toggle views dynamically between product categories like Dairy, Baking Goods, Frozen Foods, and Meat.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Demonstrated
* **Tool:** Power BI Desktop
* **Data Transformation:** Power Query (ETL process, data cleaning, and data type adjustments).
* **Data Modeling & Analytics:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for calculating custom metrics and KPIs.
* **UI/UX Design:** Canvas configuration, custom side-navigation filter panels, brand-aligned color palettes (Blinkit Yellow/Green theme), and dynamic visual tooltips.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights Derived
1. **Tier 3 Dominance:** Deep regional penetration in Tier 3 locations leads total revenue distribution, outpacing major Tier 1 hubs.
2. **Core Categories:** Perishables (Fruits & Veggies) and Snack Foods form the foundational anchors of gross sales value, making them critical inventory optimization priorities.
3. **Format Efficiency:** Supermarket Type 1 formats drastically outperform smaller grocery setups, bringing in over **\$787K** of the gross \$1.20M value.
